Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

gita-shorts is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Gita Saaram YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission, youtube.upload, solely to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

Other users

The app has no other users and does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone. It sets no cookies and does no tracking.

Data storage

The only credential kept is the owner's own access token, stored privately and used only to upload videos. The owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the Gita Saaram YouTube channel.
